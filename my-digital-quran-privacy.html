My Digital Quran Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy describes the current release and should be revised whenever the app's permissions, third-party services or data practices materially change.

Overview

My Digital Quran is designed as an offline Quran reading application. It does not require a Zynco Apps account for its core features.

Information we collect

Zynco Apps does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell or share personally identifiable information through the current version of My Digital Quran.

Information stored on the device

The app may store information locally to provide its reading features, including:

  • Bookmarks and saved reading references
  • The user's last reading position
  • Theme, display and reading preferences
  • Other application settings

This information remains on the user's device unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

Internet access and third-party services

The app's core reading features are designed to work without an internet connection. The current policy assumes the release does not use advertising networks, behavioural analytics, account sign-in or cloud synchronisation.

Children's privacy

The application is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Policy changes

Zynco Apps may update this policy when the application's features, permissions or data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to zyncoapps@gmail.com.
