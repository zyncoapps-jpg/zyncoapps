What Pocket Vault handles

Pocket Vault lets you save item titles, categories, notes, custom details, dates, documents, photos, files and secrets that you choose to add. You may import files or images using Android's picker, capture a photo using the camera app, or create a password-protected backup.

Where data stays

Pocket Vault stores vault data and copied attachments in the app's private storage on your device. It does not require an account, operate a sync service, or automatically send vault contents to Zynco Apps. The Android app does not request Internet access and contains no ads or analytics SDK.

When you explicitly share a note or save an exported backup using another app or storage provider, that destination may process the material under its own privacy practices. Pocket Vault does not control files after you export them.

Security

You choose either an app PIN or device unlock before entering the app. Sensitive secret values are encrypted within app storage. The app blocks screenshots and recent-app previews on Android. Exported backups are encrypted with the password you choose.

Ordinary item metadata and copied attachments are stored in app-private storage but are not separately encrypted by Pocket Vault. Protect your device screen lock and backup password.

Retention and deletion

Items stay in Pocket Vault until you move them to Trash and permanently delete them, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and device transfer for app data are disabled. Uninstalling the app removes its private local data under normal Android behavior.

An exported backup remains wherever you saved it; delete that copy separately if you no longer want it.

Policy changes

Zynco Apps may update this policy if Pocket Vault's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
