FAQ

Before you purchase.

Is this a Google Sheets product?

Yes. The complete Ultimate Apps experience is designed for Google Sheets and uses bound Apps Script for guided actions and automation.

Does it connect live to Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com?

No. You enter booking and operating data in your own workbook. It is not a channel manager or live booking-platform integration.

Can I manage multiple properties?

Yes. The system includes multi-property setup, Property P&L and channel analysis.

Can I amend or cancel bookings?

Yes. Version 1.2.0 includes guided amendment, cancellation and reinstatement workflows with overlap checking and turnover reconciliation.

Why does Google ask for authorization?

The automation runs inside your own copy and needs permission to perform actions such as updating workbook data, creating triggers, generating reports and making backups.

Is this tax or accounting software?

No. It is an operations and management-reporting tool and does not replace professional tax, legal or accounting advice.
