DECKZERO Privacy Policy

This page explains how the current version of DECKZERO accesses local audio and stores music-player preferences.

Last updated: August 2026. This policy describes the current release and should be revised whenever the app's permissions, third-party services or data practices materially change.

Overview

DECKZERO is an offline music player designed to browse and play supported audio already stored on the user's Android device.

Information we collect

DECKZERO does not require a Zynco Apps account and is not intended to sell or share personally identifiable information.

Device media access

To discover and play local music, the app may request access to audio or media files. That access is used to display the local library and play user-selected tracks.

The current policy assumes audio files remain on the device and are not uploaded to Zynco Apps.

Local app information

The app may store local preferences and library-related information, such as:

  • Favourite tracks
  • Playback and interface preferences
  • Recently selected or viewed items

Playback and notifications

DECKZERO may use Android media-session and notification functionality to provide playback controls while music is playing. These features support local playback and do not create a Zynco Apps user profile.

Analytics, advertising and accounts

The current policy assumes the application does not use advertising networks, cloud file uploads, behavioural tracking or mandatory user accounts.

Policy changes

Zynco Apps may update this policy if DECKZERO's permissions, third-party services or data practices change. The latest version will be published here.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to zyncoapps@gmail.com.
