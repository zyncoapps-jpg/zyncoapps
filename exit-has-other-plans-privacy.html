Exit Has Other Plans Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Zynco Apps publishes Exit Has Other Plans. This policy explains how the Android game handles information and how to contact us.

Information stored on your device

The game saves campaign progress, collected badges, room records, failure counts, and audio and motion preferences locally. It does not require a player account and does not operate a developer analytics or player-profile server. Android application backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove the local save.

Advertising

The Android game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded advertisements for clues and occasional advertisements between completed rooms. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising performance information to deliver, measure and protect advertising. IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location.

The information processed and ads served depend on the SDK, your region, device settings and privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details, including Google's handling and retention of information.

Your choices

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertising requests are allowed. When privacy options are available, you can reopen them from the game's Settings → Ad choices. Device-level advertising controls are also available through Android settings.

Rewarded ads are optional. Clues become free after five failed attempts in the current session. Normal gameplay and retries do not require a rewarded ad. The campaign works offline; advertising requires an internet connection.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access for gameplay. Its advertising integration uses network access and may use Android advertising identifiers where available and permitted.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email support, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and maintain necessary support records. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any information we must retain for legitimate legal or security reasons.

You can delete game progress by clearing the application's data in Android settings. We cannot recover or remotely delete a local save from a developer server because the game has no such server. For information processed by Google, use the privacy resources and controls linked above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game's data handling changes. The date above identifies the current version.
